# FAB Padel Junior Championship 2026

One-day youth padel tournament by **FAB Padel Club**, held on **Saturday, 19 December 2026**. It also launches the **FAB Padel Club Academy**.

**Quick facts:** U-14 (Court 1) and U-18 (Court 2), 8 pairs each. Group stage matches are first to 4 games; semi-finals and finals are first to 6. Registration is Rp 700,000 per pair. The prize pool is Rp 10,000,000.

| Item | Assumption |

## Running-the-Day Tips

1. **Call matches early:** treat times as "not before". If a match finishes early, call the next one straight away to build a buffer.
2. **Scoreboard runner:** keep group tables updated live on a whiteboard or screen at the desk so families can follow the standings.
3. **Pre-match call:** call the next pair 10 minutes before each slot.
4. **New balls:** open a fresh tin for every semi-final and final.
5. **Tie-break sheet ready:** have the group ranking rules (Info & Rules, section 7) printed at the referee desk.

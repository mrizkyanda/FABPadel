## 5. Tournament Format

### 5.1 Structure per category
Each category (8 pairs) is played on its own dedicated court:

- **U-14 → Court 1**
- **U-18 → Court 2**

**Group stage:** 8 pairs are split into **Group A and Group B (4 pairs each)**. Every pair plays the other 3 pairs in its group (round robin), for **3 guaranteed matches per pair**.

**Knockout stage:** the top 2 pairs from each group qualify for the semi-finals:

| Match | Pairing |
|---|---|
| Semi-final 1 | Winner Group A vs Runner-up Group B |
| Semi-final 2 | Winner Group B vs Runner-up Group A |
| Final | Winner SF1 vs Winner SF2 |

There is no third-place play-off. Both losing semi-finalists share 3rd place.

### 5.2 Seeding & draw
- The organiser will seed the **top 2 pairs** in each category based on known ranking, tournament results and coach assessment. They are placed in different groups.
- All other pairs are placed by **random draw**.
- The draw and full match schedule will be published on **Wednesday, 16 December 2026** and explained at the technical meeting.

## 6. Scoring Rules

### 6.1 General
- Matches are played under standard padel rules (FIP rules) unless stated otherwise in this document.
- **Golden point** applies at deuce (40–40): one deciding point is played, and the **receiving pair chooses** which side to receive on.
- Pairs change ends after the 1st game and then after every 2 games (odd games).

### 6.2 Group stage: first to 4 games
- The first pair to win **4 games** wins the match.
- At **3–3**, one **deciding game** is played. Its winner wins the match 4–3.

### 6.3 Semi-finals & Final: first to 6 games
- The first pair to win **6 games** wins the match.
- At **5–5**, a **tie-break** is played. The first pair to reach **7 points with a 2-point margin** wins it, and the match is recorded as 6–5.

### 6.4 Warm-up & timing
- Warm-up is **max 3 minutes** on court before each match.
- Rest at changeovers is **max 60 seconds**. There is no rest after the first game.
- Match times are **"not before"** times. A match may start earlier than scheduled if the court is free and both pairs are ready. Players must stay close to the court and listen for calls.

---

## 7. Group Ranking

Each match won = **1 point**. Pairs are ranked by:

1. Number of matches won
2. If **two** pairs are tied: **head-to-head** result between them
3. If **three or more** pairs are tied: **game difference** (games won minus games lost) across all group matches. Once only two pairs remain tied, head-to-head decides.
4. Total games won
5. Coin toss by the Tournament Referee

## 8. Match Conduct Rules

### 8.1 Punctuality & walkover
- Players must check in by **07:15** on match day.
- A pair that is not ready on court **10 minutes** after its match is called loses by **walkover**.
- In the group stage, a walkover is recorded as a **4–0** win to the opponents.
- A pair that withdraws or is disqualified during the group stage has all its remaining matches recorded as 4–0 walkovers. Results already played stand.

### 8.2 Retirement
If a pair retires during a match, the opponents win the match. The score is recorded as the games already played plus the games needed to complete the match.

### 8.3 Injury & medical
- One **medical time-out of max 3 minutes** per player per match, for an injury that happens during play.
- An on-site medic will be available throughout the day.
- The decision of the medic or Tournament Referee on whether a player may continue is final, for the player's safety.

### 8.4 Coaching
- Coaching is allowed **only during changeovers**, from **outside the court**.
- Coaching during points, or from inside the court, is not allowed and may be penalised.

### 8.5 Equipment & attire
- Proper sports attire is required.
- **Non-marking padel or tennis shoes** are required. Running shoes are not allowed, to protect the court surface.
- Padel rackets only (no tennis or beach rackets).
- Match balls are provided by the organiser. New balls are used for every semi-final and final.

### 8.6 Hydration & breaks
- Players must bring their own water bottle. Drinking water refills are provided.
- Players should eat and rest properly between matches. Parents are asked to make sure their child has food during the ISHOMA (rest, prayer and lunch) break and the rest windows.

### 8.7 Officiating
- Each match is supervised by a referee or court official.
- Line calls follow padel fair-play practice. When in doubt, the official decides.
- **The decision of the Tournament Referee is final** on all matters during the tournament.

## 9. Code of Conduct

FAB Padel Club is committed to a **safe, respectful and positive** environment for every young player. This code applies to **players, parents, coaches and supporters**.

**Expected behaviour**
- Respect opponents, partners, officials, staff and spectators
- Shake hands (or racket tap) before and after every match
- Accept officials' decisions without argument
- Encourage positively, never criticise or pressure players

**Not tolerated**
- Abusive, offensive or discriminatory language or gestures
- Throwing or hitting rackets or balls in anger
- Any intimidation of players or officials
- Parents or supporters interfering with play or officials

**Penalty scale (players)**

| Offence | Penalty |
|---|---|
| 1st | Official warning |
| 2nd | Point penalty |
| 3rd | Game penalty |
| 4th or serious misconduct | Disqualification |

Parents, coaches or supporters who breach this code may be asked to leave the venue. Their pair may be penalised if the behaviour continues.

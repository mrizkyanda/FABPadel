## 2. The idea

**Purpose:** FAB Padel Club is developing the next generation of Indonesian padel players. This is our first junior tournament, and the day the FAB Padel Club Academy is born.

**Campaign line (pick one, or propose better options in the FAB voice):**
- "The Next Generation Starts Here"
- "Future Champions, Starting Today"
- "Where Juniors Become Players"

**Key messages, in order of priority**
1. Real tournament for juniors: proper format, referees and prizes
2. Rp 10.000.000 prize pool
3. Limited to 8 pairs per category, so register early
4. The launch of the FAB Padel Club Academy, led by Coach Kurnia Junaedi

**Audience:**
- **Primary:** parents of junior padel players aged 9–17 in Jakarta. They are the decision-makers who register and pay.
- **Secondary:** the junior players themselves, coaches, schools and the Jakarta padel community.

**Tone:** energetic, aspirational and proud, but safe and trustworthy for parents. Celebrate effort, sportsmanship and growth, not only winning. Avoid aggressive or "battle" language, because the players are children.

**Language:** captions in **Bahasa Indonesia** (casual but polite, suitable for parents). Short English headlines are fine, for example the event name and campaign line. Write every caption in both Bahasa Indonesia and English so the club can choose.

## 3. Visual direction for the Key Visual (KV)

- **Subject:** young padel players in action: a dynamic smash or volley, celebration or teamwork between partners. Show **both boys and girls, aged about 10–17**, in sporty, modest attire, on an indoor padel court with glass walls.
- **Mood:** bright, optimistic and premium-sporty. It should feel like the start of something.
- **Graphic elements:** padel court lines, ball trajectory and motion streaks. Hint at "rising" or "growth", for example upward movement or a sunrise light, to express the next generation.
- **Do not:** use real, recognisable people or the logos of brands that aren't sponsors, or depict anything unsafe for children. Avoid cluttered layouts.
- **Image generation tip:** generate the **background or hero art without small text**. All text (dates, prices, contact details) will be added and edited in **Canva**, so leave clear space for the headline, the date block and a sponsor logo strip at the bottom.
- **Sponsor logo strip:** reserve a bottom band for sponsor logos in this hierarchy: **Owning Sponsor** (largest, and also in the event name as "[Brand] FAB Padel Junior Championship 2026"), then **Main Sponsor**, then **Prize Pool Sponsor**, then Supporting and Official Partners. Use grey placeholder boxes until the sponsors are confirmed.
